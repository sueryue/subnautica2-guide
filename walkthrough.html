Main quest

The blackbox trail, step by step

The story advances by recovering blackboxes and following NoA objectives. Here's the route, with distances from the Lifepod.

  1. Fix Digestion first. Swim ~150 m north (along the black cable) to the glowing Angel Comb and touch its pink bulb — you can't safely eat local food until then.
  2. Anita Gottstahl's Blackbox — early objective that confirms your bearings and unlocks local flora/fauna as usable food.
  3. Chap Sovann's Blackbox — in a cabin by a tall stone pillar (southeast).
  4. Quaker's Blackbox — in a flooded complex ~400 m north; also pick up the Cicada and Compartment blueprints nearby.
  5. Ruby's Blackbox — ~370 m northeast, guarded by the dangerous Marrowbreach predator.
  6. Wander's Blackbox — needs the Sonic Resonator to clear infected biofilm and destroy bloom cankers.
  7. Angel Comb / Heat Tolerance — cure the infected Angel Comb to unlock swimming in extreme heat (opens the hotter biomes).
  8. Tadpole Pens — investigate the pens, get locked out, recover Zip's Blackbox, then return with the access code.
  9. Alien Ruins — cross the Trench (Collector Leviathan territory) and reach the ruins to learn what happened to the colonists.
