Co-op & Multiplayer

Dive with up to three friends

Subnautica 2 is built as a single-player experience you can optionally take online — co-op supports up to 4 players and shared progression.

👥

Single-player first

Unknown Worlds built the game to work perfectly solo. Co-op is an added layer, not a separate mode that hollows out the campaign.

🌐

Up to 4, cross-platform

Sessions support a party of four across all PC (Steam, Epic) and Xbox Series X|S players in the same dive.

🔁

Solo ↔ Co-op

You're not locked at world creation. A single-player save can be opened into a co-op session anytime — the world keeps running.

📡

Shared progression

Inventories, storage, PDA database entries, and blueprints are shared — scan one fragment and the whole team unlocks it.

Why shared blueprints matter

Classic co-op survival forces every player to re-grind the same tech tree. Subnautica 2 removes that friction: one player scanning a wreck benefits everyone. It makes splitting up to scan different wrecks a genuinely efficient strategy — and pairs perfectly with the planned Mobile-Base Submarine, a large shared vehicle suited to a four-person squad working the Abyssal Trench.

Customizable base color & lighting (see Base Building) shine in co-op — a whole crew shares one expressive habitat.

How co-op actually plays

🤝

Share loot by standing close

Walk up to a teammate and your inventories sync — hand off resources and gear without dropping a single container. Perfect for feeding the base builder mid-dive.

🏗️

Anyone can build

Every player can place and edit modules in the shared habitat. Split the crew: one harvests while the other expands corridors and power.

💤

Downed? Respawn at the Biobed

Die and you wake at the base Biobed — your respawn point. The Biobed Upgrade is a permanent, squad-wide boost, so build one early and the whole crew benefits.

🕊️

No PvP, no friendly fire

Co-op is purely cooperative by design — you can't hurt each other, so experiment freely with flares, tools and the Tadpole.

🧭

Pick your explorer

Every session opens by choosing from a roster of explorers — a cosmetic/identity pick that carries across solo and co-op saves. Choose together so the crew feels like a real dive team.

Co-op tips from the community

Tag-team the scan game: one player clears a wreck while the other hauls loot, because blueprints are shared the instant anyone scans. Keep the Tadpole parked near the dive site as a shared safe room — its hull eats predator hits so the squad can regroup inside.
