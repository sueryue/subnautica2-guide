Resources

What to hoard, and where

Every raw material in Early Access with its abundance and the best places to farm it. Titanium is the universal currency — grab it everywhere.

Rare & advanced materials

Subnautica 2-exclusive resources

These don't exist in the first game — know where to find them before you build late-game gear. Compiled from GameRant, Polygon and community guides.

🪨

Celestine 天青石

The rarest known resource. Currently found only in The Mesa, growing on rock walls. Use rocks as cover from the Collector Leviathan overhead. Needed for the Modification Station and the Tadpole Depth Module Mk1.

💠

Conduit Crystal 导管水晶

Deep, advanced alien-tech material found near the Alien Ruins — required for late-game alien devices.

🪐

Troilite 陨硫铁

Late-game mineral. The Metal Farm can duplicate it — but you need 10 to start the loop.

🦷

Creature Enamel 生物釉质

White-pronged deposits on dark rock, mined with the Sonic Resonator — southeast of the Alien Ruins.

🧪

Necrolei Cyst 死灵囊

From the Sea Anemone Forest (west of Old Habitat). The source of Strong Acid.

🔋

Acidic Raion Pouch 酸性雷恩囊

Green orbs on purple plants. Used for Basic Batteries and acids.

🎈

Lucifer Rotsac 荧光腐囊

Crafted into Rubber (Air Bladder, fins). Pick it by hand from Cradle Rootshoot tops.

🩹

Medical Gel Sac 医疗凝胶囊

From Acid Raions. Restores +10 HP.

🏗️

Plasteel Ingot 塑钢锭

Titanium + Lithium. Used for the Scout Ray Chassis and the High Capacity Air Tank.

The two rules that save trips

① Silver is the rarest mineral in Early Access — it sits mostly near blackboxes and bloom clusters; look for green anemone-like plants as markers. ② Large veins need the Sonic Resonator — small and medium nodes can be hand-harvested, but big Silver/Gold/Quartz deposits require it, so don't panic when a big node won't break early on.

Respawn mechanics

What comes back, and what doesn't

Early Access resource nodes respawn on a timer — but a few are truly finite. Know the difference so you never strip a biome you can't refill.
