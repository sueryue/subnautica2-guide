SN1 → SN2 resource conversion hints

Basic ores map 1:1 across both games, so the Load SN1 reference preset is a safe planning baseline for them:

  • Titanium, Lead, Quartz, Glass, Copper, Lithium — same name & role in SN1 and SN2.
  • Lubricant & Enameled Glass — building consumables present in both titles, but per-piece costs differ; treat SN1 figures as a rough estimate only.

SN2 piece-by-piece building has no published exact costs — always confirm against your own scanned blueprints.

Quick base-building tips

  • • Power comes in three tiers: Solar Panels early (surface daylight), Hydro Turbines in ocean currents (mid), Thermal Plants at vents (late, near-limitless). Bridge the gaps with a Battery Terminal.
  • • Build your first base near the Lifepod, then a second at Red Grass Mesa — current-swept and sheltered, ideal for Hydro Power.
  • • You need a Tadpole Dock (plus a Vehicle Fabricator) before you can build or upgrade the Tadpole — prioritize it once you have fragments.
  • • Piece-by-piece construction is the headline change: place individual panels and stretch surfaces into any shape. Plan your corridors before you commit titanium.
  • • Watch hull integrity: too many connected modules without Foundation or Support Pillars cause flooding or collapse. Add supports as you expand.
