Patch notes

What changed — and what changed in this guide

Subnautica 2 is in Early Access (launched May 14, 2026, 5M+ players). Unknown Worlds ships regular content updates, so this page tracks each live patch and what it means for your dive.

EA 1.2 · Aug 19, 2026

Buddy System — co-op becomes real co-op

The Buddy System update turns Subnautica 2's optional multiplayer into a genuine team experience: talk, trade, revive, and read the room.

📻

Proximity voice chat

The first in-game voice chat. Teammates sound clear up close, but drift apart and voices switch to a crackling radio-style signal that breaks up with distance — a deliberate touch that Unknown Worlds creative director Anthony Gallegos says "adds to the tension."

🧑‍🚀

Two new divers: Grace & Jegna

Two additional playable characters join the explorer roster, shipped with two new suit colors so a four-person crew can tell each other apart at a glance. Matching outfits remain an option if you'd rather be a synchronized team.

🫁

Player revives

When a teammate goes down, you have a short window to reach them and share oxygen to bring them back — a proper second chance that rewards sticking together in Leviathan territory.

🎒

Inventory sharing & trading

Walk up behind a teammate and open their inventory to swap items directly — no more dumping gear on the seabed. Feeding the base builder mid-dive is now a two-button exchange.

👏

Emotes

A quick menu lets you cheer, clap, or vent frustration at your crew — non-verbal coordination for when voice chat isn't your style.

📍

Tracking Tag

A short-range beacon you can throw into the environment or stash in a locker. Die while carrying one and it extends your black-box signal, making lost gear easier to find. HUD icons also got smaller by default and scale up on hover to cut beacon clutter.

What else shipped in 1.2

Beyond the co-op features, the patch improved pinned recipes, base-builder clarity, and squashed a long list of bugs — including a Hydroturbine bug that stopped power production after ~120–160 minutes, Tadpole docking glitches, and the Collector Leviathan spinning when it grabbed or threw a Tadpole.

EA 1.1 · July 8, 2026

Adaptive Measures — evolve, fight, and move faster

The first major Early Access update. It reworks the Biomod system, adds two new Biolabs, and hands you real combat and movement tools.

🧬

Biomods: 4 → 6 from Biolabs

Biolabs now unlock a total of six Biomods, up from four. Two new Biolabs were added — one in Coral Gardens and one in the Axum Ruins. See Adaptations for the full unlock map.

🔬

Passive slots via the Bioscanner

Scanning creatures with the Bioscanner now unlocks additional passive Biomod slots, and the UI shows exactly how many scans each slot needs plus your running Bioscan total. Creative mode unlocks every Biomod by default.

🏃

Sprint on land & in your base

You can now sprint out of the water — on the surface and inside your habitat — with a remappable sprint key. Default walk speed was lowered to balance the new burst of speed.

⚔️

Creatures now react to you

Most fauna flee when hit by the Survival Multitool instead of instantly retaliating, and a Sonic Resonator hit leaves them briefly dizzy. Blight creatures also telegraph their passive-to-aggressive transition more clearly.

🏚️

Wrecks: new routes & oxygen puzzles

Wreck interiors got extra traversal and oxygen-based puzzles, with more consistent door/button visuals and moved rewards to match. Bring spare air and a plan.

📦

Personal storage (crafting-exempt)

A new storage buildable that deliberately does not feed nearby crafting systems — finally a place to stash keepsakes without them being auto-consumed by a recipe. The World Tree Relic also arrived as a buildable decoration.

Quality-of-life in 1.1

A FOV slider joined Settings, audiologs stopped auto-playing (they now notify and replay from the PDA Databank), dialogue got a priority system so callouts don't overlap, and the Vehicle Dock / Fabricator got placement and readability improvements.

Roadmap

What's coming next

Unknown Worlds has teased the next big milestone, plus an experimental branch to preview it early.

A larger expansion is on the way, adding a brand-new region/biome, new creatures, new resources, new tools, a new vehicle/chassis, new progression, and more story. An opt-in Experimental branch opened on Steam (Aug 12, 2026) to preview upcoming content — expect this guide to grow with it.
